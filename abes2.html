ABES Engineering College (ABES EC) is a reputed educational institution located in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. The college focuses on providing quality education in the fields of engineering, technology, and management. It offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programs that help students develop strong academic knowledge along with practical and technical skills. The campus provides facilities such as modern classrooms, well-equipped laboratories, a library, computer facilities, and spaces for sports and extracurricular activities. ABES EC encourages students to participate in technical events, workshops, seminars, hackathons, cultural activities, and various student clubs. These activities provide students with opportunities to improve their communication, teamwork, leadership, problem-solving, and professional skills. The college also emphasizes practical learning and industry exposure so that students can understand how the concepts they learn in classrooms are applied in real-world situations.
