Our Courses

ABES Engineering College offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering, technology and computer applications. The courses are designed to provide students with technical knowledge, practical skills and industry exposure.

Undergraduate Courses

  1. B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering

    This course focuses on programming, software development, databases, computer networks and emerging technologies.

  2. B.Tech in Information Technology

    Students learn about web development, databases, networking, information systems and modern IT technologies.

  3. B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering

    This course covers electronics, communication systems, embedded systems and digital technologies.

  4. B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering

    The course provides knowledge of manufacturing, machine design, thermodynamics and mechanical systems.

Postgraduate Courses

  • M.Tech
  • Master of Computer Applications (MCA)
  • Master of Business Administration (MBA)
